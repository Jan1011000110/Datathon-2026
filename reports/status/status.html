Where we are

  • To do now, in the hard-mode tab (attempts expire at 21:00): submit submissions/h2/submission.zip to the ranking. Optionally submissions/h1/nov_fill/submission.zip as a comparison point.
  • h2 is confirmed on the server (probe on the free validation, 19:00): it built fully, 0 fallbacks, 1.4 ms per step (about 3 s for a full run), same numpy and pandas as ours. Every assumption checked holds: lists shrink only in the last two hours, the state of charge is unrounded, the night-time load is frozen, solar is ×12.
  • h2 = the combined agent, packaged and cleared: 20.11 on our folds (21.27 / 20.37 / 18.70), 0 infractions, about 5 s per run, falls back to h1's logic if anything fails on the server. That is 95 % of the ceiling, about 22.4 on the public test by the usual scaling.
  • h3 is being packaged (target 19:50): h2 plus a second-round signal forecaster and a planner that doubts the signal forecast. Together +0.075 on our folds (20.19); 22 of 33 test-like weeks improve.
  • What is still missing (about 1.1 points): the level of the price/CO₂ signal 3 to 8 hours ahead (about 0.5), which obs does not seem to contain, and the net load of the hour being decided (0.30), mostly on weekday mornings.
  • The server's obs is confirmed by our probe (18:50): 3 price/CO₂ values, the six readings, battery 6,000 / 1,500, first hour 24 Sep 2025. Exactly as documented.
  • Both signals are public data (checked on three train days): the price is REE's published PVPC hour for hour, and the CO₂ column is exactly 0.37 × the share of emitting generation REE publishes. So the test weeks' true signal could be embedded in an agent, and our agent with a perfect signal would sit where the top two teams are (about 98 % of the ceiling). Our agents do not do this; whether it is allowed is a question for the organisers.
  • Anomalies get little weight now. The organisers' statement that nobody had reached the private maximum was wrong. We keep what is certain or free (no crashes or infractions, the real layout, holiday weeks, fallbacks) and stop tuning guards for faults.
  • Leaderboard (19:07): ten teams in a 0.4-point band at 23.06–23.46. That band is where our own agent lands when given the true signal (about 23.2), and the signal is public, so that is the most likely source of the gap. The alternative, that the public test is easier than we assume, is settled by our first h2 submission: about 22.4 means the gap is real, 23 or more means we are in the pack.
