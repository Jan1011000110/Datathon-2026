What we found out about the leaderboard

The two halves leak into each other

The public board scores the first 84 hours of each test week, the private ranking the last 84. Whatever charge is in the battery at hour 84 crosses the line, so points can move between halves without a better model.

You can see it on the board

We changed one setting and our public score went from 23.32 to 24.25. On Sunday three teams were above 27. Our estimate of the best possible score on those hours is about 24.4.

The signal is public data

Price and CO₂ are Red Eléctrica’s published series, so the real values for the test weeks can be downloaded. We told the organisers and didn’t use them.

Our two picks: h4, the general agent, and h3p, which plans each week on its own and tops the battery up before the private half starts. Its first lines say so. Scoring whole weeks would remove the effect.
