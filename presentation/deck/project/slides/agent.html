This is the whole agent on one slide; the next three slides open each box. Every hour it forecasts three things: the campus load, the solar output, and the price and CO2 beyond the three hours it is given. Each forecast comes with quantiles measured on weeks the model did not train on. Then it plans: an exact dynamic programme over the next 36 hours, run on nine scenarios of net load and twenty error paths of the signal. It is model predictive control: we re-plan every hour and apply only the first command. Finally the command is clamped just inside the physical limits, and if any part fails there are three levels behind it: the previous agent, a simple table-based agent, and zero. We judged every change by the closed-loop score on three validation folds, days 1 to 7, 8 to 14 and 15 to 21 of each month, with every model fitted outside the fold it is scored on.
