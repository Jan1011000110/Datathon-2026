What came from us, not from the model

Barcelona’s own holidays

The model treated holidays in general and skipped the ones only Barcelona has. Validation couldn’t see them either: none falls in the training days, so the numbers said a calendar wasn’t worth shipping. We knew the campus shuts for La Mercè and Whit Monday. Three dates typed in: +0.23 on the board.

Chasing the public/private split

Scores on the board kept moving without better models. We didn’t crack it ourselves, but we decided that was the question that mattered and put the work there: probes instead of tuning. The answer: the public board is the first 84 hours of each test week, the private ranking the last 84.

Is the signal just public data?

We asked for the check. Price and CO₂ are Red Eléctrica’s published series. We told the organisers and didn’t use it.

Don’t build on a rumour

We had planned around broken sensors in the test after a remark from the organisers. We checked it, it was wrong, and we dropped that track.

What counts as fair

Real test-week values inside the agent: no. Topping the battery up before the private half: yes, and the code says so in its first lines.

This slide is the part that didn't come out of a model or a validation score; it came from us. First, holidays. The model handled holidays in a general way and skipped the ones that are specific to Barcelona. And validation couldn't help: none of Barcelona's local holidays falls in the training days, so on paper a calendar was worth almost nothing and the recommendation was not to ship it. But we know when a Barcelona campus is closed: La Mercè in September and Whit Monday in May, plus Sant Joan. We typed in three dates and gained 0.23 on the board. Second, the split between the public and the private test. We saw scores moving on the leaderboard without anyone having a better model. We didn't work out the answer ourselves, but we decided that was the thing to understand, and spent submissions on probes instead of tuning. Three smaller calls underneath: asking whether the price and CO2 signal was simply public data, which it is; not building on a remark from the organisers that turned out to be wrong; and deciding what counts as fair.
