Things we saw in the data

The load meter stops at night

From 23:00 to 05:00 the reading repeats, so we know the night demand exactly. At 06:00 it jumps, for example +383 kW on Mondays.

Cold panels mean a sunny day

If the panels are colder than the air before sunrise, the sky is clear (correlation −0.56). We use it in the solar model.

The battery is bigger than the campus

In 87 % of the hours we discharge, the battery could give more than the campus uses. The extra is given away.

Errors are not symmetric

If we predict too little sun we waste energy. If we predict too much we lose almost nothing.

Better forecast, same score

Four times we made the price and CO₂ forecast more accurate and the score did not change. What matters is the average level of the next hours.

No test week in August

An agent that only acts in August got exactly 0. In July it got 0.53. So the test ends on 31 July.
