How much to discharge

Discharge one more kW only if

w · η · P(net load > d) > v

w is what a kWh is worth now, η the efficiency, v what it is worth if we keep it.

In words: what it earns now, times the chance that the campus really uses it, has to be more than what it is worth later.

At night the demand is stable and we discharge what we forecast. At 13:00 solar is uncertain, so it can be much less or much more.

(about 20 s) This is the rule the optimiser follows. It discharges one more kilowatt only if what it earns now, times the chance that the campus really uses it, is more than what the energy is worth later. So at midday, when solar is uncertain, it is more careful.
