Before building anything clever we asked how good any agent can be. A linear program with perfect foresight gives the ceiling: 21.26 points on our validation weeks, which are days 1 to 21 of each month, with every model fitted outside the week it is tested on. Then each step of our agent is measured as points lost against that ceiling. The first plain planner loses 2.45. The final agent loses 1.07, so it captures 95 percent. We also know what is left: about half a point is the price and CO2 signal beyond three hours, and perfect load knowledge would add only 0.19.
