THE PROBLEM

Decide every hour, see three hours ahead

6 MWh

Battery of 6,000 kWh, up to 1,500 kW in or out, 90 % round-trip efficiency.

3 h

Price and CO₂ are known for this hour and the next two. Standard mode shows 24.

×12

Solar output is twelve times the real plant’s, so the campus often has surplus.

70/30

Score: 70 % CO₂ avoided, 30 % bill saved, minus 0.20 per hour that breaks a limit.

Everything beyond three hours has to be forecast: price, CO₂, the campus load and the solar output.

Hard mode changes the problem. In standard mode the agent sees 24 hours of price and CO2, and a plain optimiser is almost perfect: more than 30 teams are tied there. In hard mode it sees three hours, the battery is three times larger, and the solar plant is twelve times larger, so the campus is often in surplus. The agent has to forecast four things and decide under uncertainty.
