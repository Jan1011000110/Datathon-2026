This is the result we are proudest of. Our validation weeks are days 1 to 21 of each month, but the test weeks are the last seven days. Looking at the calendar, three test weeks start on a holiday: La Mercè on 24 September, Pentecost Monday on 25 May and Sant Joan on 24 June. In the training data the campus draws about half its usual load on holidays. On these regional and local holidays the price keeps its weekday shape, so the agent charged overnight for a working day that never came. We typed three dates into the load forecaster, predicted a gain of 0.20 from our validation, and the server gave 0.23. It also told us something we could not check from the data: the campus does close on Barcelona's local holidays.
